BOROMETER — CHANGELOG
=====================

v1.9
----
  - Fight now shows the damage as a glowing ring. Each skill is an arc around
    the big number, sized by its share of the damage, with the hit types on a
    thin outer rim and the names outside. Point at an arc or a row and both are
    highlighted, while the middle gives that skill's damage, hits, crit rate and
    biggest hit
  - Click an arc and the list beside the ring opens that skill with its hit
    types and closes the one that was open. A second click closes it again
  - “Replay the fight” lets the skills race each other in the ring: the whole
    fight in about ten seconds, at 1×, 2× or 4×, and you can scrub. Mechanics
    show up on the way, and at the end the first three places stand with one
    sentence
  - In a party the ring shows the members inside and their skills outside, your
    rank in the middle and the share per member below. A click opens a member's
    ring; Esc or “‹ Group” goes back
  - Critical heavy is ember orange now and easy to tell from critical (gold)
    everywhere, also with colour blindness
  - Borometer recognises mechanics: stretches of a boss fight where you keep
    hitting but your hits do hardly any damage, at about the same time in at
    least 80 % of five or more of your pulls. The Analysis lists them as a
    finding of their own, the curve shows them hatched, and those seconds no
    longer count as your gap or your weakest stretch. Borometer does not guess
    the cause
  - King Khanzaizin: after 20 seconds without a hit a new fight begins, so wipes
    are no longer glued into one fight. Other bosses are unchanged
  - Records, the trophy in the icon bar, is an album: the highest hit overall at
    the top, then pages for raid, practice dummy, field bosses and dungeons by
    stars. Each boss has a medallion with best DPS, strongest hit and first
    fight, and bosses you have not fought yet stand as empty outlines. The first
    time you open it, Borometer reads the 60 newest logs in your log folder;
    stopping loses nothing
  - Below the big number, a neutral line compares this pull with the last one,
    such as “+8 % vs. last pull”. When all fights fall on one day or two days in
    a row, History lines the pulls up one after another with their time. The
    fight picker puts fights in a row at the same place under one header, and
    Party shows the board first
  - On first start the app shows a tour: six short steps, from the log folder to
    “Report a bug”. You can skip it, and “Show the tour” in the settings brings
    it back, also in the browser
  - New in Settings › Info: “Check for a new version at start”. The switch stays
    off until you turn it on. Then Borometer asks GitHub once per start, and if
    a newer version is out, a quiet note in the status bar and in Info links to
    the release page. Nothing is downloaded or installed; GitHub sees your IP
    address and the time
  - Borometer is open source under the GPL. Code, downloads and issues are on
    GitHub at B0R0AK/Borometer, and the license notes are in English too
  - Where a game image is missing, Borometer draws its own mark instead of an
    empty circle. In the compact window, rows without an icon now line up with
    the others

v1.8
----
  - Weeklies in a new look: the week as a band from Thursday 10:00 to Thursday
    10:00 with a line for now, the raid as a 3×3 grid with the bosses of its
    three wings, every merchant item with its in-game icon and a ring that
    fills, and a progress ring on every character
  - The compact window follows the full view: it shows the fight you have open
    there and changes with it. If it cannot follow, it says why instead of “No
    log”
  - The compact window stays compact: it has a maximum size, and the ⋯ menu,
    dialogs and notices show in full – the window grows for them and shrinks
    back. See-through only tints the ground; skills, names and numbers stay
    solid
  - Vegamor is recognised: no more control codes in the fight name, the whole
    boss event is one run with each part as its own fight, and Vegamor has its
    own picture
  - Damage at the practice dummy is right again: a skill id is never read as
    damage any more
  - “Report a bug” sits in the status bar at the bottom right instead of the
    settings
  - New icons for the practice dummy and for Falling Flower

v1.7
----
  - Borometer looks and works like a companion app: an icon bar on the left, the
    fight picker in the title bar (‹ › and Ctrl K), a status bar at the bottom
    and the content as one calm surface. Nothing is stretched on a large screen
  - Fight: every skill with its bar under its name, the curve and a verdict with
    two reasons beside the table, and the in-game icon of the skill core (34 of
    35 cores)
  - Rotation: the whole fight as a timeline to play at 1×, 2× or 4×, as a bar or
    as a sequence, and the stacked damage over time right under it on the same
    axis – one playhead runs through both. In a party, the rotation of each
    member
  - Earlier days: the fight picker turns back to earlier log files in the game's
    log folder. While you look at an older day, live logging pauses; “Today”
    picks it up again. On Start, “Recently opened” opens the file
  - Compact is its own small window over the game on Windows 11, and the big
    window stays where it is – on a second screen, say. A normal Windows window
    over the game, not an overlay in it: Borometer never touches the game
  - Builds keep your Questlog links with a small overview from your own logs:
    fights, best pull and median per build. One click opens the build on
    Questlog
  - Weeklies: a checklist for up to six characters with the weekly and daily
    tasks. It resets on Thursday at 10:00 and every day at 10:00 German time,
    and every character can hide, rename or add points
  - Settings are one scrolling page with a jump bar; “Show in Explorer” opens
    the game's log folder
  - The rotation trainer, the evening and the “All casts” canvas are gone.
    Everything you saved stays where it was

v1.6
----
  - A new tab, Progress, answers one question: are you getting better, and
    where does it come from? Everything in it stays on your PC
  - Against your best pull: one click in the fight header compares this fight
    with your best on the same boss – at the practice dummy with your best of
    the same length, 60, 120 or 180 seconds. If this fight is your best, it is
    set against your second best. Numbers, skills and the rotation side by
    side
  - Your builds: Borometer recognises a build by its weapon pair and the
    skills that make 80 % of your damage, and shows per boss the median of
    every fight with it. You name a build once and may add a planner link;
    there is nothing else to enter
  - Rotation trainer: after each pull, up to three sentences on where your
    first 30, 45 or 60 seconds went differently from your best pull – fewer
    casts, a later first cast, another order, a pause. Where the boss could
    not be hit, it says so and names nothing there. In Rotation, “Best pull as
    outline” draws your best pull over your own casts
  - Evening: the bosses of a raid night, the best pull per boss, the
    difference to last week and who improved – from the party logs, or from
    your own fights without a party. Nobody is named from the bottom
  - Share as image: a fight, the party board or the evening as a PNG to save
    or to copy to the clipboard. Borometer draws it from its own numbers – no
    screenshot, no game art – and says whose names are in it before you copy

v1.5.1
------
  - The installer wears the app's dark look on every page, not only on the
    first and the last. It no longer asks for whom to install – Borometer
    always goes into your own user folder, without admin rights – and it skips
    the license page
  - Running a newer installer over Borometer updates it in place. Uninstalling
    says what it removes and that your settings and saved fights stay where
    they are

v1.5
----
  - Live logging is one switch: “● Live” while it runs, “○ Live off” when it
    doesn't, and a click turns it on or off. It keeps its label at every
    width, in compact mode and at 200%, and the taskbar button toggles it too
  - The damage table grows with the fight instead of scrolling in a third of
    the window. When the window is narrow or zoomed, the header plate gets
    smaller first, so the table stays in view
  - The strongest skill no longer wears gold: gold is for the number and for
    what you selected. In the light theme nothing glows any more, and a bar
    that fills the whole column fades out so it doesn't look like a selected
    row
  - The Analysis starts with one verdict: what cost you the most in this
    fight, or that nothing did. “Weakest three seconds” no longer points at
    the last seconds of the kill
  - Every action has one place: “Save fight” only next to the fight, the
    rail's footer is “Filter and fight file”, and the tab is called Party like
    the button. How-to text sits behind a “? Help” button instead of above the
    charts
  - Compact mode without a fight shrinks the window to a strip again. Notices
    no longer cover a button, follow the language switch, and an Undo notice
    stays for its full time

v1.4
----
  - Borometer wears the real Windows frame. Minimise, maximise with its snap
    layouts, close, the border to resize by, the corners and the shadow come
    from Windows; the header is the title bar. The buttons follow the theme
    and the UI size
  - Compact sits on Acrylic on Windows 11: the game shows blurred behind the
    overlay while the figures, names and gold stay solid. See-through now sets
    how much of the game shows through the ground, not how faded the numbers
    are. On older Windows it fades as before
  - The taskbar knows Borometer: a gold dot on the icon while live logging
    runs, and two buttons in the preview, Compact and Live logging
  - A maximised window is maximised again after compact mode instead of
    running past the edge of the screen
  - The app's own local server only answers the app itself. A web page in a
    browser can no longer send it commands or change the log folder

v1.3
----
  - Click-through overlay: Ctrl+Shift+D lets the mouse pass through the
    compact window into the game, and the same keys take it back. From the
    full view it makes the whole overlay at once — compact, on top,
    click-through. The strip says so with an amber edge and the key
    combination
  - Narrow windows and 200 % zoom: the fight list folds into one line with a
    button to open it, so the big number stays at the top. Clicking a fight
    folds it again
  - The installer and the portable exe look like the app: dark pages, the word
    mark, their own words in German or English. The portable exe shows a start
    image while it unpacks. Switching tabs fades in 140 instead of 260 ms
  - One word for one thing: “Live logging on/off” everywhere instead of “Live
    log”, “LIVE OFF” in the compact strip instead of a bare “OFF”, and “fights
    file” instead of “runs file”
  - Comparing two different bosses no longer paints the difference red or
    green, and the breakdown of where it came from starts folded. Hit counts
    are grouped (1,201) and differences in the comparison are in k form like
    every rate beside them
  - The light theme reaches readable contrast on the selected fight and on the
    click-through note. The sticky table header covers the rows under it, the
    compact focus ring no longer lies on the neighbouring numbers, and compact
    prints the damage as 32.54M
  - The analysis questions stand one step above their answers, and every
    figure stands once — “Eye of Ventius” over “91% of damage” instead of the
    91 twice. “Own peak” in the timeline no longer blows a lane with almost no
    damage up to full height
  - The start page carries “Open logs” and “Live logging” once instead of
    twice, and the setup steps are numbered quietly instead of with four
    glowing buttons. Opening compact mode folds open table rows

v1.2
----
  - Compact mode reads the fight too. Under the big number stands a short
    line – which skill carried it – and how this pull did against the last
    one on the same target, with its sign and in green or red
  - Compare Fights says what it measures against, and one button swaps the
    reference. Two different bosses get no winner, because every boss takes
    damage differently
  - Hosting a party says what it opens, right under the button: a port on
    this PC that answers party traffic only and only with the code – or
    nothing at all when a party server is set. A button that cannot be
    pressed yet no longer glows
  - The timeline has a key for its two bands: seconds with no hit, and the
    weakest three seconds
  - The analysis questions stand above their rows, and the verdict number is
    set in the same face as every other big number
  - The table header is one tab stop; the arrow keys walk the columns and go
    into the rows. The switches above the table tell a screen reader which
    one is on
  - Switching tabs after scrolling down lands at the top of the new tab.
    Rows in compact mode are 24 points tall, big enough to hit
  - German reads like German again: “2 Kämpfe aus dem Beispiel gelesen”
    instead of the button's label, and the fight list heading is one
    sentence

v1.1
----
  - Borometer runs on Electron now instead of Python. It brings its own
    window and needs neither WebView2 nor an installed browser; what you see
    and click is the same as in 1.0
  - Every release comes three ways: an installer with Start menu entry and
    uninstall, a portable exe, and a zip to unpack. Installed, your saved
    fights go to Documents\Borometer; portable, next to the exe
  - Your settings stay where they are. All three read the same file in
    %APPDATA%\Borometer as the Python version did, so nothing needs to be
    set up again
  - The version in the window was stuck at v0.64. It now comes from the
    build itself

v1.0
----
  - The interface is new. A type scale with real steps, quieter surfaces,
    denser rows, and one set of controls instead of eight kinds that only
    looked alike
  - Skills have icons now, 313 of them, with the weapon behind each one. You
    recognise what you used by the picture instead of the name
  - The timeline is a curve. Instead of 168 columns, one area with a lane per
    skill — a click takes a lane out
  - The rotation has a bar of its own: what you used and when, on a time
    axis, with the hit ranges next to it
  - Analysis has a head and sections, and tells you where your weakest window
    was
  - Compact mode became an overlay: a narrow strip over the game that fades
    while you are not looking at it
  - Compare fights, Host party and the start screen have been reworked
  - Bosses stand in the fight list with their picture
  - Both charts can be driven from the keyboard
  - In the light theme gold is gold again. On a light surface it used to turn
    brown
  - Skill cores have a mark of their own. A skill core is not a skill but an
    item that replaces one
  - Two Borometers no longer take the same port. The second window used to
    show the first one's page
  - The saved theme comes back after a restart, TnL included
  - In compact mode the sort column sat on the wrong row, and the damage
    total was cut off
  - Ramus is Ramux, Grimturg and Exodus are field bosses, and Tevent's Hunger
    (Enraged) does not replace a skill after all
  - Borometer is under the GPL v3 from now on. The source comes with it

v0.59
-----
  - There is a second download: a folder instead of a single file. The
    single file unpacks itself into a temp folder every time it starts, and
    that is one of the commonest reasons an antivirus flags it — the code
    has nothing to do with it. In the folder version nothing unpacks. Unzip
    it somewhere and run BoroMeter.exe inside. Same app, 150 files instead
    of one
  - Under the Timeline it says what a click does again: click a burst to
    zoom to it, click it once more to come back. The control has been there
    all along — the text was trimmed in 0.56 and it went with it
  - The dark theme carries exactly the opacities it had before the light
    theme was rebuilt. Nine alpha steps had ended up on three settings, and
    the drift measured 5 of 255 on 1px hairlines. There are 25 settings now,
    one per original value. Checked across all 674 rules: none differ. The
    light and TnL themes were not touched — 0 of 4,561,200 pixel channels

v0.58
-----
  - New tab: History. Every combat log in your folder, read once. On this
    machine that is 482 fights across 176 targets, 61 of them fought more
    than twice. Pick a target and every fight you had with it is there, one
    bar each in the order you fought them, best in gold. Fellinex sat
    between 150k and 481k and the app never showed it
  - Under the fight header, one line: where this fight stands among your
    others on that target. “Your best of 8 on this target”, or “20 % below
    your median of 5”. Gold only when it is the best. Nothing at all under
    two fights — “your best of one” is not information
  - The history does not do its own maths. Every file goes through the same
    four steps as loading one, and the numbers come out of the same
    function, so a fight has one number and not two. Checked by reading the
    same file both ways: 13 fights against 13, names and rates identical to
    three decimals
  - The gold button was below AA where its descenders sit. A label occupies
    22 % to 85 % of a button's height, and the g in “Open logs” reached into
    the dark foot of the gradient: 3.92:1. The amber stop moved from 62 % to
    85 %, so the worst point under the text now reads 5.66:1. Same shape of
    mistake as the blue button in 0.57, this time on the main button
  - 23 lines of stylesheet that matched nothing are gone, all of them left
    over from 0.56. Each one was checked on its own first, and two
    candidates survived that check: “.org” and “.w3” were www.w3.org inside
    the select arrows, and “.wbar” is alive in the weapons table
  - One limit worth knowing: switch the game's language and the same enemy
    turns up twice in the History, once per spelling. Skills are matched by
    their id, but a target in the log has only a name

v0.57
-----
  - The blue “Join with this code” button read 2.79:1 in the light theme,
    below AA, right where the label sits. Its middle gradient stop followed
    the theme; the ink on it did not. All three stops are fixed now: 10.34 /
    7.34 / 5.14 in either theme. The “5.14:1” in the 0.56 changelog was the
    foot of the button, not the part the label is on
  - The light theme has colours of its own instead of the dark ones turned
    around. Page, cards, buttons and bars all sat within nine degrees of one
    hue, so only light against dark was left to tell them apart. The ground
    is verdigris now, 121 degrees off the gold. Gold, amber and the twelve
    skill colours did not change
  - Light surfaces are flat, a hairline replaces the lit top edge, and only
    what floats casts a shadow. A button under the mouse darkens instead of
    paling. White is kept for data
  - 67 colours in the stylesheet were hardcoded to the dark theme's gold, so
    the light theme could never repaint them — hence a gold hairline round
    every input and a pitch-black shadow under every menu. 35 are settings
    now. Gold that means something stays gold; gold that is only an edge
    turns grey
  - A third theme: TnL, after the game's own interface. Indigo ground,
    magenta for what is selected, gold for numbers — the game keeps those
    two apart and so does this. Pick it in the ⋯ menu under Colours
  - Three of the twelve skill colours are one step brighter in TnL. On
    indigo they fell below 3:1; the hue is the same, so a skill keeps its
    colour when you switch themes
  - The dark and light themes came through unchanged: 0 of 4,561,200 pixel
    channels differ. The one thing you will see in all three is a wider ⋯
    menu, because a fourth button had to fit

v0.56
-----
  - A practice target is never merged into one fight any more, whatever the
    checkbox says. It is known by name: Practice Dummy, Übungspuppe and
    Arkeum Statue for Training — 14,300, 6,091 and 236 rows across the 89
    logs on this machine. Measured on a built file, the same dummy twice
    with a 20-second pause between the runs: one fight of 43.9 s in two
    parts before, two fights of 11.9 s each now. The rate was already right,
    because a non-boss only counts the seconds actually fought; what changes
    is the row. Every attempt stands on its own, and the dummy is the one
    target people use to read attempts against each other
  - The hint under “Connect boss phases” no longer recommends something that
    stopped being true in 0.53. It said: turn this off to keep every attempt
    on a practice dummy separate. Since 0.53 a merged fight that is not a
    boss divides by the time actually fought — only the row is merged, not
    the rate. Following that advice put a different number on screen from
    the one on the party board: one Deluzhnoa pull fell into 594k and 545k
    instead of the 360k standing there. The new text names what the box does
    in both positions, and the minute that is really behind it
  - The “Attackers” button and the “Your character” field only appear when
    the loaded log really holds more than one attacker. A Throne log is one
    player's data: counted over the 89 logs on this machine, 89 times
    exactly one attacker and never two, so both controls offered a choice of
    one. They are not gone — load a whole party's log files together and
    there are several, and both come back. A table left on “Attackers” when
    the button goes switches to “Your Damage”
  - The Analysis tab answers the questions it asks. “Damage consistency”
    read “145% variation” — that is the coefficient of variation, and it
    reads like “45% above average”: 100 looks like normal and there is no
    upper bound. Where the number stood there is now the reading it was
    supposed to carry, Burst, Cooldown rotation or Steady. “How many hits
    were critical?” was answered with “87% of the damage”; now the value
    answers the question and the damage share sits in the line below. Four
    findings are gone — cast rhythm, opener against the rest, strongest
    three seconds, damage per cast — and so are the fifteen-second windows.
    Downtime says something when there was none, where it used to print
    nothing, and nothing reads like “not measured”
  - In the filter every field carries its own hint, in its own column. Both
    hints used to run the full width under both fields, so reading “Default
    3” left you guessing which of the two numbers it was about. The fields
    are called “Split fights after (s)” and “Minimum fight length (s)” now.
    The long paragraph about the bug report is out of the filter; the report
    itself is still in the ⋯ menu. The button in the rail summarised itself
    as “Everyone in log | 8s / 3s” — the first half says the same thing in
    almost every log, the second repeats two numbers that sit one click away
    in their own fields. It says “Filter” now, with a line above it naming
    what is behind it
  - “Open logs” in the header is a menu with two entries: Throne logs and
    party logs. A saved party log used to be loaded at the foot of the party
    tab, where nobody went looking for it, and both are files you open.
    Saving a party log stays on the party tab. The pill beside the menu says
    “Live log off” instead of “not watching”
  - No party can be started or joined without a character name. Both buttons
    used to run into nothing and answer with a message; now they take a
    press only once the name is there, and Join once the code is there as
    well. Spaces do not count as a name. Join carries a colour of its own so
    the two paths do not look alike — gold leads, blue accompanies, the role
    --info already has in this app. The foot of that gradient started at
    #31628c, where the ink on it measured 2.89:1; it is #4e8cbb now, at
    5.14:1
  - A “Developer mode” checkbox in the ⋯ menu, off by default. It brings
    back three things that stand where they always stood and are merely
    switched off: the Log setup tab, “Export CSV” in the fight header, and
    “Paste a list instead” on the Weapons tab. Hiding them outright was the
    worse answer — for the one per cent who need them they were then not
    there at all. The tick survives a restart: “devMode” is on the
    launcher's list of what it will write, the same list that swallowed
    “splitAfter” for half a year, and all eleven settings were checked
    against the running launcher. Log setup also shows itself whenever a
    column it is needed for is missing, developer mode off or not, because
    several messages in the app point at that tab
  - A fight is what you save and a run file is what collects them, and the
    labels finally say so in the same words. “Save as run” is “Save fight”
    on both buttons, the list in the rail is “Saved fights”, and the two
    buttons in the filter are “Save run” and “Load run”. The dialog asks
    “Give this fight a name:” instead of suggesting you name it after your
    build. The Compare tab is called “Compare Fights”, and its heading no
    longer says “Compare two runs” on a tab that takes two to four
  - The Timeline and the Rotation have no headings over them any more, and
    no tally under them. Under the Timeline stands what you can do with it:
    Ctrl and the mouse wheel zoom, and once you are zoomed in you drag the
